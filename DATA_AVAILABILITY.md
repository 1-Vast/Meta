# Data Availability

The repository records governed manifests, hashes, split metadata, and experiment results. Large
third-party datasets, pretrained model snapshots, protein embedding banks, molecular graph banks,
and caches are not redistributed through source control.

Local processed assets are indexed by their `manifest.json` files under `dataset/processed/`.
Numerical claims must cite the corresponding result and manifest hashes. A local asset path is not
evidence that the asset may be redistributed; upstream licenses and source terms still apply.
