# Required Repair

Resolve the target mismatch with one theorem for one explicitly named target.

For a risk-optimal target, the theory must prove or explicitly assume sufficient conditions for an observable, continuous target selector `g*:Z->C`, and derive a calibration inequality of the form

```text
operator error <= Phi(excess population risk),  with Phi(t) -> 0 as t -> 0.
```

Alternatively, if the target is the canonical observable operator, the claims must be restricted to canonical imitation and must not be presented as task-risk learning of the risk-optimal operator. The imitation labels and their construction from inference-time or training-time observables must then be typed explicitly.

Until one of these routes is proved without switching targets, TF-10 through TF-15 do not establish the claimed trainable operator foundation.
