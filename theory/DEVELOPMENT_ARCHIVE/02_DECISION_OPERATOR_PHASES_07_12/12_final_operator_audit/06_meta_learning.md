# Meta-Learning-Specific Audit

## Result

`PASS FOR INFORMATION FLOW; LEARNING CLAIM REMAINS SCOPED`

## Tier separation

- Existence: a marked-law ideal object exists by regular conditional
  probability, and the observable canonical estimator exists as a construction.
- Identification: `Pi_obs` determines the outer identified operator
  `M^dagger`, not a unique latent lift.
- Learning: finite tasks estimate `M^dagger` under S1-S6; sampling does not
  remove the latent-lift identification width.

The theory does not use existence as a proof of identification or learning.

## Information flow

No latent mark is supplied to the estimator. Historical input is
`H_N in T^N`, and endpoint indicators are measurable functions of each
observable task record and its identified set. The marked law is used only to
type ideal latent conditionals and prove outer coverage.

The current query `Q_*` is an explicit evaluation index, not a hidden label.
The population arm receives the current observation only through the declared
context `kappa(O_*)`; the identification arm separately consumes current
support. No future response or hidden task identity is used.

The learned, observable target remains `M^dagger`. Any claim of convergence to
a particular marked lift would be invalid, and Phase 11 correctly avoids it.
