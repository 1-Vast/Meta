# Complete Mathematical Stack

## Layer 1: identification

For observations `O`, the frozen map is

$$
I:O\mapsto I(O)=\{f\in\mathcal F:f\text{ is compatible with }O\}.
$$

For a finite query set `Q`, the decision-relevant identified pushforward is

$$
J_Q(O)=e_Q(I(O))
=\{(f(x))_{x\in Q}:f\in I(O)\}.
$$

Archive uncertainty is handled by the Phase-6 union of archive-consistent
families; auxiliary information restricts each candidate by a label fiber.
Nothing in Phase 7 changes this set.

## Layer 2: decision

A decision context declares an action space `A`, loss `L`, and comparison
criterion. The decision layer is

$$
D:(J_Q(O),\Delta,\mathcal A,L)\mapsto A^*,
$$

where `Delta` is the additional comparison information: a monotone completion
of dominance, concretely generated by a law, ambiguity class, robust criterion,
reference, or declared tie-break.

The separation is mathematically preserved:

$$
I(O)\text{ and }J_Q(O)\text{ are invariant under every }\Delta.
$$

Population information can reweight compatible members but cannot remove them
from the identified set.

## Required corrected composition

For a generic decision context, the no-population fallback must be the
criterion-typed set-robust problem

$$
R_{\rm set}(J;\mathcal A,L)
=\inf_{a\in\mathcal A}\sup_{v\in J}L(a,v),
$$

not automatically the scalar Chebyshev center and half-diameter. The latter is
the special case `A=R`, `L(a,v)=|a-v|`.

The complete stack should therefore be

$$
\text{history}\to\Pi_n
\to\mathcal Q(O)
\quad\text{and}\quad
O\to I(O)\to J_Q(O),
$$

followed by

$$
A^*=\arg\min_{a\in\mathcal A}
\sup_{\mu\in\mathcal Q(O)}\int L(a,v)d\mu(v).
$$

If the argmin is not a singleton, the output must remain set-valued or consume a
declared tie-break. This correction is not yet fully supplied by Phase 7.
