# Evidence Ledger

Immutable index of every retained experiment. `RESULT.json` /
`COMPARE_*.json` in the named leaf is numerical authority; this table is
navigation and verdict only. `python -m scripts.audit_research_record`
recomputes the arm metrics, the k=0 Pareto frontier, the `meta_test` seal
classification and every recorded checkpoint sha256 directly from those
artifacts.

**`meta_test` appears in two different senses in this table.** Stages 4, 6
and 7 report the pre-double-cold `bindingdb_ki_main_v0` `meta_test`
(42 targets / 6-7 components), which was legitimately consumed under the
older protein-only-cold protocol. The double-cold
`bindingdb_ki_double_cold_v1` `meta_test` (22 targets / 10 components) has
**never been opened** and is physically sealed. Rows from R1 onward refer to
the sealed split; earlier rows do not.
