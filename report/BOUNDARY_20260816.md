# Reachable Boundary on the Double-Cold Protocol (consolidated, 2026-08-16)

This document states, with complete evidence, what the current data and the
tested method families can and cannot reach on
`bindingdb_ki_double_cold_v1` (meta_train 5,643 cells / 346 targets;
meta_val 41 targets / 19 components; meta_test 22 targets / 10 components,
physically sealed and never opened). Every number below has a leaf
`RESULT.json` / `COMPARE_*.json` authority under `report/meta_fewshot/`, and
is recomputed from those artifacts by
`python -m scripts.audit_research_record` (see `report/RECORD_AUDIT.json`).
No claim here is an impossibility proof for DTA; it is the measured boundary
of this protocol, this feature set (sequence + 2D graph, no complex
coordinates), this budget (1200 steps) and the tested families.

**Every number in this document is a `meta_val` development record**, three
seeds, on the population that selected the configurations. Nothing here is
confirmed: the double-cold `meta_test` has never been opened. See
"Two different `meta_test` populations" below — this project's older reports
contain a `meta_test` that is *not* this one.

## The k=0 Pareto frontier, correctly defined

A frontier is a set of **whole configurations**, not an interval formed by
taking the best value of each metric from a different model. Over the two
preregistered primary k=0 metrics (MSE down, CI up), exactly three arms are
non-dominated:

| rank | arm | k=0 MSE | k=0 CI | what it buys, what it costs |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | **B3** (R3R4 full method) | **2.055** | 0.531 | best MSE and best calibration (1.131); worst ranking of the three |
| 2 | **C2** (R9, cliff w=2) | 2.119 | 0.548 | the interior compromise; best k=0 cliff sign (0.621) |
| 3 | **A0** (incumbent) | 2.149 | **0.580** | best ranking; worst MSE of the three |

Everything else in the arm table is dominated — including C1, the arm that
holds the best k=5 activity-cliff record.

Three consequences that the earlier "frontier 2.055-2.119 / CI 0.548-0.580"
phrasing obscured:

1. **No single trained model achieves both** the best MSE and the best CI.
   The earlier phrasing read B3's MSE together with C2's, and C2's CI
   together with A0's, describing a model that does not exist.
2. The frontier is only 0.094 MSE wide (2.055 to 2.149, 4.4%) and 0.049 CI
   wide. **None of the MSE differences against A0 is resolved** by a
   component-level paired bootstrap; the CI regressions are the resolved
   part of the trade.
3. The preregistered Z1 target (-10% vs A0, i.e. 1.934) lies outside the
   frontier entirely and has never been approached from any direction.

## The activity-cliff record, stated at its true scope

The best activity-cliff ordering on record is **k=5 cliff sign accuracy
0.782, arm C1, Stage R9** — three seeds, double-cold `meta_val`, evidence
grade **development**. It is a `meta_val` development record only. Three
qualifications travel with it and must not be dropped:

* C1 is **Pareto-dominated** on both primary k=0 metrics (MSE 2.235 vs A0's
  2.149; CI 0.562 vs 0.580). The cliff record does not come from an
  admissible configuration.
* The comparison points that make it look large are on the same population:
  A0's 0.675 and the parameter-free Morgan/Tanimoto prior's 0.716 (Stage
  R0, and that one was measured on the *older* protocol's exact-ligand-free
  pairs, so it is an indicative rather than matched comparison).
* It was never measured on the sealed `meta_test`, so it is not a
  generalization claim.

What the R9 dose response does establish, and this part is mechanism
evidence rather than a leaderboard number: **the cliff-ordering ability
comes from the shape-first training itself, not from the activity-cliff
pair weight.** C1 (weight 1) beats B1 (weight 4) on global CI (0.562 vs
0.535) *and* on cliff pairs (0.606 vs 0.577 pooled).

| population | where | size | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bindingdb_ki_main_v0` `meta_test` | Stages 4, 6, 7 | 42 targets / 6-7 components | **consumed**; reported and used for cross-arm comparison under the older, protein-only-cold protocol |
| `bindingdb_ki_double_cold_v1` `meta_test` | R1 onward | 22 targets / 10 components | **sealed, never opened**; physically excluded by `QPSMPData(include_meta_test=False)` |

Statements like Stage 6's "F wins on `meta_val`, the incumbent wins on
`meta_test`" and Stage 7's "`meta_test`, descriptive only" refer to the
**older, consumed** population. They carry no information about the sealed
double-cold confirmation split, and they are not evidence of generalization
under the double-cold protocol, whose split has zero exact-ligand, scaffold,
protein-component and document overlap with training.

`scripts/audit_research_record.py` enforces the separation mechanically: it
classifies every `RESULT.json` as double-cold-sealed (explicitly, via the
R5 seal record), double-cold-sealed (implicitly, pre-R5 artifacts that
record only `meta_val`), or older-protocol. As of this audit: **75 explicit,
15 implicit, 140 older-protocol, 0 violations.**

## What was falsified, each with its measured cause

1. retrieval priors (R0): help only exact/near-duplicate ligands;
2. Cartesian/3D encoders (Stage 5): 0/17,717 legal inputs;
3. seven query-specific few-shot channels across three families (R6a
   saturating gate, R6b additive correction, R7 rho gate + four earlier):
   deployment-inert while disturbing calibration under every objective
   tested;
4. level/shape routing on the incumbent trunk (R11): the incumbent's
   calibration lives in the interaction branch; routing it away breaks the
   trunk;
5. the shape variance term as the margin-compression cause (R10): falsified;
6. margin-ranking loss form as the actionable lever (R12): CI +0.003 only;
7. the direct MLP interaction-head shape (R13): gate-blocked at Stage 1 —
   the shape branch collapses under the variance term on the synthetic
   interaction task. **18 gates: 16 pass, 2 recorded as `xfail`**
   (`tests/test_shape_direct_synthetic.py`, thresholds unmoved).

## The boundary, stated precisely

- **k=0 MSE**: no trained model exceeds B3's 2.055 (-4.3% vs A0,
  unresolved); the Z1 target of -10% (1.934) has never been approached. The
  remaining k=0 error is roughly half target-level calibration and half
  within-target shape; the best calibration achieved is B3's 1.131 (vs A0
  1.236) and the best shape term is B1's 0.896 (vs A0 0.913) — again, two
  different models.
- **within-target ordering**: the trained CI ceiling observed is ~0.56 for
  the factorized families and 0.580 for the incumbent; the pair audit shows
  small-gap and mid-similarity strata sit near their noise floor (sign
  accuracy 0.48-0.55) while cliff pairs reach 0.61-0.78.
- **k=1 query-specific adaptation**: not achieved by any tested mechanism;
  the retained useful few-shot behavior is the Tanimoto-weighted residual
  shift (k>=2) and the scalar level shift (k=1).
- **protein specificity at k=0**: wrong-protein gaps cross zero for every
  arm; specificity is resolved only at k>=2 in the routed arms.

## What would be required to move the boundary (honest prerequisites)

1. more within-target measurement signal — the 5,643-cell training block
   and ~9-21 ligands per target bound what any inductive model can learn
   about ligand ordering under label noise;
2. a calibration source that exceeds the incumbent's trunk without the
   routing tradeoff (e.g., external protein representations reported as
   external data, or a non-routed level design);
3. a shape readout that does not collapse under regression objectives
   (the R13 failure) and has the inductive bias the bilinear form
   provides without its supervision leak.

`meta_test` remains sealed; it opens once, only for a candidate that passes
every preregistered meta_val gate.
