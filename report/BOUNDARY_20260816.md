# Reachable Boundary on the Double-Cold Protocol (consolidated, 2026-08-16)

This document states, with complete evidence, what the current data and the
tested method families can and cannot reach on
`bindingdb_ki_double_cold_v1` (meta_train 5,643 cells / 346 targets;
meta_val 41 targets / 19 components; meta_test 22 targets / 10 components,
excluded; see the seal-strength note). Every number below has a leaf
`RESULT.json` / `COMPARE_*.json` authority under `report/meta_fewshot/`, and
is recomputed from those artifacts by
`python -m scripts.audit_research_record` (see `report/RECORD_AUDIT.json`).
No claim here is an impossibility proof for DTA; it is the measured boundary
of this protocol, this feature set (sequence + 2D graph, no complex
coordinates), this budget (1200 steps) and the tested families.

**Every number in this document is a `meta_val` development record**, three
seeds, on the population that selected the configurations. Nothing here is
confirmed: no double-cold `meta_test` label entered any fitting, selection or reported metric. See
"Two different `meta_test` populations" below — this project's older reports
contain a `meta_test` that is *not* this one.

## How much of this table is retraining noise

*(Added 2026-08-16 after R14 screening.)* The incumbent configuration was
retrained here under an identical setup and identical seeds (`A0repro`,
`meta_fewshot/stageR14_screening_20260816/`). Against the frozen checkpoints
it differs by **0.058 in k=0 MSE (2.0911 vs 2.1488) and 0.051 in within-target
`r` (0.162 vs 0.213)**. CUDA training here is documented as not bitwise
deterministic.

The entire frontier spread below — A0's 2.149 down to B3's 2.055 — is
**0.094, about 1.6x that same-config noise.** Read the frontier accordingly:
its points are separated by roughly one to two retraining standard
deviations, none of its MSE differences was ever resolved by a component
bootstrap, and **A0's 2.149 is one draw rather than a constant.**

## The k=0 Pareto frontier, correctly defined

A frontier is a set of **whole configurations**, not an interval formed by
taking the best value of each metric from a different model. Over the two
preregistered primary k=0 metrics (MSE down, CI up), exactly three arms are
non-dominated:

| rank | arm | k=0 MSE | k=0 CI | what it buys, what it costs |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | **B3** (R3R4 full method) | **2.055** | 0.531 | best MSE and best calibration (1.131); worst ranking of the three |
| 2 | **C2** (R9, cliff w=2) | 2.119 | 0.548 | the interior compromise; best k=0 cliff sign (0.621) |
| 3 | **A0** (incumbent) | 2.149 | **0.580** | best ranking; worst MSE of the three |

Everything else in the arm table is dominated — including C1, the arm that
holds the best k=5 activity-cliff record.

Three consequences that the earlier "frontier 2.055-2.119 / CI 0.548-0.580"
phrasing obscured:

1. **No single trained model achieves both** the best MSE and the best CI.
   The earlier phrasing read B3's MSE together with C2's, and C2's CI
   together with A0's, describing a model that does not exist.
2. The frontier is only 0.094 MSE wide (2.055 to 2.149, 4.4%) and 0.049 CI
   wide. **None of the MSE differences against A0 is resolved** by a
   component-level paired bootstrap; the CI regressions are the resolved
   part of the trade.
3. The preregistered Z1 target (-10% vs A0, i.e. 1.934) lies outside the
   frontier entirely and has never been approached from any direction.

## The activity-cliff record, stated at its true scope

The best activity-cliff ordering on record is **k=5 cliff sign accuracy
0.782, arm C1, Stage R9** — three seeds, double-cold `meta_val`, evidence
grade **development**. It is a `meta_val` development record only. Three
qualifications travel with it and must not be dropped:

* C1 is **Pareto-dominated** on both primary k=0 metrics (MSE 2.235 vs A0's
  2.149; CI 0.562 vs 0.580). The cliff record does not come from an
  admissible configuration.
* The comparison points that make it look large are on the same population:
  A0's 0.675 and the parameter-free Morgan/Tanimoto prior's 0.716 (Stage
  R0, and that one was measured on the *older* protocol's exact-ligand-free
  pairs, so it is an indicative rather than matched comparison).
* It was never measured on the sealed `meta_test`, so it is not a
  generalization claim.

What the R9 dose response does establish, and this part is mechanism
evidence rather than a leaderboard number: **the cliff-ordering ability
comes from the shape-first training itself, not from the activity-cliff
pair weight.** C1 (weight 1) beats B1 (weight 4) on global CI (0.562 vs
0.535) *and* on cliff pairs (0.606 vs 0.577 pooled).

### The seal's strength before 2026-08-16

`QPSMPData.include_meta_test` **defaulted to `True`** until 2026-08-16, so the
seal held only for callers that opted out. The three trainers hardcode `False`
and were always correct. Six analysis scripts took the default and therefore
parsed and indexed the sealed cells; one of them, `r14_dispersion_audit.py`,
additionally wrote a seal claim its own code did not implement.

**No recorded number is affected, and this is demonstrated rather than
asserted**: re-running that audit under the repaired seal reproduces 105 of 105
numeric A0 fields bit-identically. The precise claim the current contract can
defend is that no double-cold `meta_test` label entered
a metric, and has informed no decision — *not* that it was untouched at the
process level during that period. Full scope, verification and residual
exposures: `tools/research/a2_readiness_v2/GOVERNANCE_INCIDENT.md`.

### "Sealed" means metric-unconsumed, not untouched

Three pre-R5 runs (`stageR3R4_level_shape_20260815/A0_incumbent_seed*`) were
produced by a trainer that evaluated the double-cold `meta_test` **before the
seal was authorised**. Those numbers were moved into
`SEALED_meta_test_DO_NOT_OPEN.json` sidecars and the `test` field replaced by
a pointer. The values exist on disk.

The honest claim is therefore: the double-cold `meta_test` has **never been
opened, never been read into a metric, and has informed no decision** — the
seal sidecars assert exactly this, and every run from 2026-08-16 onward is
prevented from reaching it by the fail-closed default plus a mandatory
written authorization. That is logical exclusion after parsing, not physical
isolation: the corpus is one all-label artifact, so every sealed label is
still decompressed and parsed on every load and then discarded
(`SPLIT_ISOLATION_SPEC.md`). It is *not* true that `meta_test` has never
been computed, and it is *not* true that it was unreachable in every process
before 2026-08-16. The audit tracks both as their own states rather than
folding them into either "sealed" or "violation", because the claims differ and
only the weaker ones are supported.

## The R14 Phase 2 correction: what "shape" actually measured

*(Added 2026-08-16 after `meta_fewshot/stageR14_diagnostics_20260816/`, a
no-training decomposition of the same frozen checkpoints.)*

The shape term splits **exactly** into
`Var(y)(1-r²) + (sd_p - r·sd_y)²` — an ordering floor that only a better
within-target correlation `r` can lower, and an amplitude excess that any
positive rescale removes without touching any rank metric. Applying it to the
arm table above changes what several rows mean:

| arm | ordering floor | amplitude excess | k=0 `r` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **A0 incumbent** | **0.692** | 0.221 | **0.213** |
| C2 | 0.716 | 0.185 | 0.148 |
| C1 | 0.731 | 0.172 | 0.150 |
| B1 | 0.735 | 0.161 | 0.096 |
| G1 | 0.751 | 0.166 | 0.134 |
| B3 | 0.753 | 0.172 | 0.073 |

Three consequences:

1. **The plain MSE-trained incumbent has the lowest irreducible ordering
   error of every arm in the project, 8 of 8.** Every ranking-primary arm is
   worse on the quantity that enters the MSE.
2. **The cause is the training method, not the architecture.** G1 is the
   incumbent architecture with zero changes, trained shape-first: `r` falls
   0.213 -> 0.134.
3. **B1's "best-ever shape term" was shrinkage.** Its 0.017 shape gain is
   +0.043 worse ordering offset by 0.060 less amplitude. The activity-cliff
   records stand as measurements, but they are compatible with poor global
   within-target correlation, because cliff pairs are the high-similarity,
   large-gap, easy pairs.

`r` is therefore the only quantity that moves MSE and CI together, and no
tested configuration has beaten the incumbent on it.

## What was falsified, each with its measured cause

1. retrieval priors (R0): help only exact/near-duplicate ligands;
2. Cartesian/3D encoders (Stage 5): 0/17,717 legal inputs;
3. seven query-specific few-shot channels across three families (R6a
   saturating gate, R6b additive correction, R7 rho gate + four earlier):
   deployment-inert while disturbing calibration under every objective
   tested;
4. level/shape routing on the incumbent trunk (R11): the incumbent's
   calibration lives in the interaction branch; routing it away breaks the
   trunk;
5. the shape variance term as the margin-compression cause (R10): falsified;
6. margin-ranking loss form as the actionable lever (R12): CI +0.003 only;
7. the direct MLP interaction-head shape (R13): gate-blocked at Stage 1 —
   the shape branch collapses under the variance term on the synthetic
   interaction task. **18 gates: 16 pass, 2 recorded as `xfail`**
   (historical Stage 1 suite; thresholds unmoved, implementation pruned after
   verdict consolidation);
8. a protein-conditioned amplitude head (R14 Phase 2): falsified *before
   implementation* — a global rescale worsens 6/8 arms and the per-target
   optimal scale is negative in 25.2% of targets;
9. the regression-compatible within-target ranking term (R14): the alignment
   identity holds exactly, but the term is **inert at this model's operating
   point** — it supplies 1.7% of the regression term's gradient, and simply
   deleting the ranking term reproduces it to within 0.002 on every metric.
   The `1/T(p)` factor that creates the stationarity also damps the term
   wherever predictions are under-dispersed. **The loss-form axis is now
   closed**: R9 (cliff dose), R10 (variance), R12 (margin) and R14 each
   varied the within-target ranking objective and none moved the ordering
   floor.
10. the exact episodic A2 moment operator (Stage R, re-run 2026-08-16 on
    repaired fixed banks): loses to fixed Morgan/Tanimoto at every k
    (−0.042 / −0.173 / −0.217 / −0.252 at k=1/2/3/5) and its wrong-protein
    and label-shuffle controls are **unresolved** rather than inverted,
    because the operator is inert — query spread at k=5 is 0.0027 pK against
    Tanimoto's 0.2865 and a random feature's 0.4521. The earlier "resolved
    and inverted" reading was an artifact of non-nested episode banks and
    `PYTHONHASHSEED`-salted `hash()` seeding, both repaired; the superseded
    result is retained as evidence, not as a comparator
    (`tools/research/a2_exact_probe/STAGE_R_EXACT_A2.md`).
11. centered-objective protein conditioning (Stage P, 2 arms × 3 seeds,
    1,200 steps): **P1 fails at −0.0066 [−0.0545, +0.0417]**. The mechanism
    is verified working — gradient into the level branch is 8.1e-07 (float32
    zero, as the centering algebra requires) while gradient into `embed` and
    `interaction_head` rises 4.6× and 3.6× — and the objective made the
    protein response *reproducible* across seeds (cosine +0.316 vs the
    incumbent's −0.059). Its alignment with centered truth is +0.022 against
    a +0.10 threshold. Scope of the closure: *centered-objective training on
    the current `ContactGrammar`, at this budget, with sequence + 2D inputs*.
    Not a claim about protein-conditioned architectures in general
    (`tools/research/stageP_cpc/REPORT.md`).

**One earlier claim was withdrawn rather than falsified.** Stage L's
"Tanimoto points in the wrong direction on activity cliffs" is void: the probe
scored a *symmetric* similarity against a *signed* ΔY on unbalanced pairs. Under
the rebuilt balanced construction (every unordered pair contributes both
orientations) a symmetric score has **exactly zero** signed correlation by
construction — measured −0.0000 — so it has no direction to point in. The
rebuilt probe (`stage_l2_directional_sar.py`) does find a real learned
directional signal, +0.212 ± 0.011 over three sampling seeds and +0.188
[+0.052, +0.325] against a capacity-matched learned ligand-encoder difference.
But its strata invert the earlier story: **activity cliffs move from +0.379 to
−0.118** (188 pairs, 18 targets, 10 components) and the high-novelty tercile
falls to +0.052 against +0.240 / +0.245 in the less-novel terciles. The signal
is concentrated where ligands resemble what has already been seen.

## The boundary, stated precisely

- **k=0 MSE**: no trained model exceeds B3's 2.055 (-4.3% vs A0,
  unresolved); the Z1 target of -10% (1.934) has never been approached. The
  remaining k=0 error is roughly half target-level calibration and half
  within-target shape; the best calibration achieved is B3's 1.131 (vs A0
  1.236) and the best shape term is B1's 0.896 (vs A0 0.913) — again, two
  different models.
- **within-target ordering**: the trained CI ceiling observed is ~0.56 for
  the factorized families and 0.580 for the incumbent; the pair audit shows
  small-gap and mid-similarity strata sit near their noise floor (sign
  accuracy 0.48-0.55) while cliff pairs reach 0.61-0.78.
- **k=1 query-specific adaptation**: not achieved by any tested mechanism;
  the retained useful few-shot behavior is the Tanimoto-weighted residual
  shift (k>=2) and the scalar level shift (k=1).
- **protein specificity at k=0**: wrong-protein gaps cross zero for every
  arm; specificity is resolved only at k>=2 in the routed arms.
- **protein-conditioned within-target ordering, at any k**: not achieved, and
  now measured directly rather than inferred from a gap. On the incumbent
  architecture a similarity-matched **wrong** protein reproduces the correct
  protein's within-target ordering to the third decimal at every k — `A0repro`
  0.156/0.156 at k=0 and 0.334/0.334 at k=5; `CPCoverdrive` 0.149/0.148 and
  0.331/0.331 (Stage P). The k>0 ordering gain from 0.156 to 0.334 is
  therefore attributable to **ligand-side support transport, not to protein
  identity**. This is the single most constraining measurement in the record.

## What would be required to move the boundary (honest prerequisites)

1. more within-target measurement signal — the 5,643-cell training block
   and ~9-21 ligands per target bound what any inductive model can learn
   about ligand ordering under label noise;
2. a calibration source that exceeds the incumbent's trunk without the
   routing tradeoff (e.g., external protein representations reported as
   external data, or a non-routed level design);
3. a shape readout that does not collapse under regression objectives
   (the R13 failure) and has the inductive bias the bilinear form
   provides without its supervision leak.

### What Stage P changed about this list

*(Added 2026-08-16.)* Stage P was built to separate two explanations that fit
every prior measurement equally well: **(a)** no objective had ever asked for
protein-conditioned ordering, versus **(b)** the signal is not reachable from
these inputs. **(a) is now excluded** — the objective asked at four times the
weight, on every episode, through a gradient route verified to reach the
interaction path and verified to exclude the level branch, and the model
complied with a reproducible but uninformative protein response.

That moves prerequisite 2 from "a calibration source" to the more general
**"a protein representation that carries within-target ordering information at
all"**, and it weakens the standing fusion/pooling hypothesis. The Phase 3
causal audit measured a 150× attenuation from `context` to `mean_state` and
~3,400× in the protein-token Jacobian, and Conditional Stage F is authorised
if that audit "proves that **useful** protein differential reaches `context`
and is predictably destroyed by fusion/pooling". Stage P is the closest direct
test of the word *useful*, and it returns +0.022 alignment: an over-driven
objective with a 4.6× amplified gradient into `embed` extracted no ordering
information from whatever survives the attenuation. **Attenuation was measured;
information was not.** Widening a bottleneck on an uninformative channel has no
predicted effect, so Stage F's authorising condition is met only in its literal
reading and not in its intended one. Any Stage F preregistration must first
establish that the pre-fusion differential is informative — cheaply, as a
no-training probe on `meta_train` components — before spending a training
budget on the bottleneck.

`meta_test` remains sealed; it opens once, only for a candidate that passes
every preregistered meta_val gate.
