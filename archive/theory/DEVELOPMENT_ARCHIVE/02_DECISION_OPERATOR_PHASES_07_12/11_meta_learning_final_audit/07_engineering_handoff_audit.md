1. A corrected transferable domain \(M(c,Q,\gamma)\), or a formal rule embedding the complete query into \(\gamma\).
2. A joint measurable marked-task law containing the latent decision target used by the ideal operator.
3. A representation/topology/metric for \(\mathbb M\) and a measurable parameterized approximation family.
4. Uniform complexity assumptions over the full query/context/specification index, not only one finite event family.
5. A consistency or approximation theorem connecting a trainable \(A_\phi\) to the canonical population operator.
6. A precise Route-A sufficiency condition if that alternative is to support full-record conditioning.

These are theorem-level definitions and assumptions, not architecture choices. A deep model cannot be certified merely by choosing a network and training loss around the current interface.
