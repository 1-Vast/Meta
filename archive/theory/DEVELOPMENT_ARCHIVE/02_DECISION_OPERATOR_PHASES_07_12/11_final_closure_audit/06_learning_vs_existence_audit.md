# Existence, Identification, and Learning Audit

## A. Operator existence

`CONDITIONAL_PASS`

A deterministic argmin exists under compactness and lower-semicontinuity. Otherwise the valid realizable object is an \(\eta\)-argmin set, and its guarantee must be the selected action's evaluated outer risk or carry the declared \(\eta\)-slack. The final interface now states this distinction correctly.

Abstention is an ordinary action and failure is a separate report, so neither is needed to manufacture existence of an optimizer.

## B. Operator identification

`CONDITIONAL_PASS`

Given the exact joint feasible object, full declared decision context, policy type, tie-break, ranking outcome convention, and a correctly typed population ambiguity class, the set-valued decision is determined. A singleton is determined only through uniqueness or the declared \(\tau\).

At conditioning rung 3, identification of the current conditional law additionally requires a query-indexed sufficiency declaration for the actual joint record/member law. A known likelihood alone does not identify a single posterior while the prior law remains set-valued.

## C. Finite-history learning

`FAIL_AS_A_COMPLETE_INTERFACE`

The component concentration result is valid under IID/C-IID: finite historical tasks yield simultaneous event constraints, and the full-space ranking polytope correctly consumes them. But complete learning is not established because:

- the interface defines \(\mathcal H\) as a finite **set**, which discards duplicate task records and therefore the multiplicities required for frequency estimation;
- the zero-count current-fiber case is not assigned a valid confidence object;
- the conditioning theorem is not valid uniformly over the noise-kernel class as claimed;
- DC-R5's interval-separation test does not characterize common-argmin robustness;
- learning the feasibility operator remains conditional on the frozen Phase-5 tameness, stability, effectivity, and one-sided certificate assumptions.

## Separation verdict

The closure documents distinguish the three levels in prose, but DC-I2 incorrectly concludes that nothing remains to be supplied. Proofs of conditional existence and identification do not establish the complete finite-history operator claimed by the final interface.
