# The Meta-Operator Space (§3)

> **Status:** Phase-10, 2026-08-03. Supplies what the audit found missing for operator-level statements: a topology/metric on the codomain, measurability of evaluations and of $A_\phi$, and the operator typing. New results **LC-8–LC-10**, tagged **[proved] / [declared]**.

---

## 1. The operator space $\mathbb M$

**Definition LC-8 ($\mathbb M$, with topology and measurability).**
Index set $\mathcal I=C_\kappa\times\mathcal Q_0\times\Gamma_0$ — **countable** by declaration ($C_\kappa$ finite; $\mathcal Q_0$ the countable query atlas of LC-1; $\Gamma_0$ a countable declared specification atlas). For each $\iota=(c,Q,\gamma)\in\mathcal I$ the value space is
$$\mathbb V_\iota\ =\ \mathcal C\big(\Delta(\Omega_Q)\big)\times(0,1]\times\mathrm{Rung},$$
where $\mathcal C(\Delta(\Omega_Q))$ is the space of nonempty compact convex subsets of the simplex $\Delta(\Omega_Q)$ (finite-dimensional for finite $\Omega_Q$; for scalar pushforwards, of the corresponding compact law space), metrized by the **Hausdorff distance** $d_H$ induced by total variation. Each $(\mathcal C(\Delta(\Omega_Q)),d_H)$ is a complete metric space (Hausdorff hyperspace of a compact convex metric space).
$$\mathbb M\ =\ \Big\{M:\mathcal I\to\textstyle\bigsqcup_\iota\mathbb V_\iota\ \Big|\ M(\iota)\in\mathbb V_\iota;\ \text{rung-consistency};\ \text{projective coherence (LC-5)};\ \text{zero-fiber convention}\Big\},$$
with two declared topologies and their σ-algebras:
- **evaluation (product) topology** $\tau_{\mathrm{ev}}$: pointwise convergence per index — metrizable as $\sum_j 2^{-j}\,d_H(M(\iota_j),M'(\iota_j))\wedge1$ over an enumeration of $\mathcal I$; separable-metrizable; its Borel σ-algebra equals the cylinder σ-algebra generated by evaluations;
- **uniform metric** $d_{\mathbb M}(M,M')=\sup_{\iota\in\mathcal I}d_H(M(\iota),M'(\iota))$: complete (uniform limits of $\mathbb V$-valued maps; constraints closed under uniform limits); finer than $\tau_{\mathrm{ev}}$; the learnability theorem (§5) is stated in $d_{\mathbb M}$, all probability statements in the evaluation σ-algebra (the two agree on the events used, since $d_{\mathbb M}$-balls of the estimator are countable intersections of evaluation events).

**Measurability [proved].** Each evaluation map $\mathrm{ev}_\iota:\mathbb M\to\mathbb V_\iota$ is $1$-Lipschitz for $d_{\mathbb M}$ and continuous for $\tau_{\mathrm{ev}}$, hence Borel. A map into $\mathbb M$ is (cylinder-)measurable iff every $\mathrm{ev}_\iota$-composition is — the criterion applied to $A_\phi$ below. $\square$

## 2. The operator, retyped on this space

**Definition LC-9 (meta-learning operator).**
$$A_\phi:\ \bigcup_{N\ge0}\mathbb T^N\ \longrightarrow\ \mathbb M,$$
with validity $V_A$ carried from Phase 9 (multiset-typed sample use; ceiling; auditability; zero-fiber fallback) and the coverage clause now **index-complete**: rung-typed coverage per evaluation, and — under the §5 assumptions — uniformly over $\mathcal I$.

**Proposition LC-10 (the canonical estimator is a measurable $\mathbb M$-valued map). [proved]**
The forced/compatible construction — $\mathrm{ev}_{(c,Q,\gamma)}\big(A_\phi(H_N)\big)$ = the constraint polytope with endpoints $\tfrac1{N_c}\sum_i l_i(E;Q,\gamma)$, $\tfrac1{N_c}\sum_i u_i(E;Q,\gamma)$ over the event atlas, with margins and tags — has each evaluation a finite arithmetic combination of indicator functions of measurable per-task conditions (membership of the task's identified object in forcing/compatibility relations — measurable in the record), hence measurable into $(\mathbb V_\iota,d_H)$; by the countable-evaluation criterion, $A_\phi$ is measurable into $\mathbb M$. Totality (empty history, zero fibers → vacuous values) holds by construction. $\square$

## 3. Operator typing (carried exclusions)

The output is an **operator** — an element of a typed function space with validity predicates — and provably cannot be an embedding, latent vector, or task identity: the Phase-9 exclusion theorem (MC-7: decodable representations reduce to the operator; coordinate-meaningful ones contradict frozen gauge unidentifiability and dimension laws; identifiers are non-transferable lookup keys) applies verbatim to the retyped codomain, with one strengthening now available: since $\mathbb M$ carries a metric, "being an operator" has mathematical content — approximation, convergence, and consistency claims (§5) are statements in $(\mathbb M,d_{\mathbb M})$, which no unconstrained finite vector space can host without a decoding map that would itself be the operator. **[proved by reduction, MC-7 + LC-8]**
