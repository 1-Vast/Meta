## Established Findings

- Generic ligand potency is a strong baseline; assay, document, and measurement
  processes contain real structure but do not identify interaction.
- Target-specific ligand reordering exists locally: KirHub retained 222
  constructs, 34 genes, 22 families, gene-macro reversal `0.1133`, and LCB
  `0.0865`. It is not an independent multi-family affinity substrate.
- Correct aligned pocket, sequence, and target assignment carry information.
  Pooled ESM expresses coarse family signal but not reliable strict dual-cold
  ligand reordering.
- The fixed LOCK kernel is nontrivial (PSD minimum eigenvalue `2.07e-16`),
  while its BLOSUM semantics lost to aligned identity, permutation, and matched
  random PSD.
- The direct R-MAON operator has a regular exact null and passed synthetic
  calibration; this is not evidence that a real mechanism exists.
- UBSE-G0R observed-label reliability passed: 1,028 units, correct Jaccard
  `0.75`, wrong-ligand `0.50`, margin `[0.125,0.200]`, Recall@1
  `0.5604` versus random `0.0846`. It was not a deployable student.
- P0A is a target-marginal proposal only: AP `0.3159`, correct-minus-
  propensity `0.2147` (LCB95 `0.1604`), and correct-minus-shuffle
  `0.2551` (LCB95 `0.1971`).
- A1-v2 Phase A acquired 421 coordinate bodies for 459 instances without
  parsing. It did not establish a structural teacher.
