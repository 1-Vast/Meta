## 1. Established Findings

### 1.1 Claim ladder

These four claims are distinct and must be proved in order:

| level | claim | current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | LOCK/CLOCK is useful in local protein mutation landscapes | established by its source literature, outside this DTA program |
| 2 | substitution geometry is identifiable in the available DTA substrate | not established |
| 3 | the coordinate predicts target-specific ligand reordering | not established |
| 4 | the mechanism improves strict target-cold plus ligand/scaffold-cold affinity prediction | not tested on a compliant substrate |

A pass at one level never proves the next.

### 1.2 Core diagnosis

The failure is not "protein biology is useless." The current binding
constraint is the absence of a powered, source-resolved, multi-family,
endpoint-consistent set of complete or design-weighted target-by-ligand
measurements that can identify an interaction while target and ligand are both
cold.

The relevant estimand is valid:

```text
C(m;a,b) = y(mut,a) - y(mut,b) - y(WT,a) + y(WT,b).
```

It is not a new information source. REWIRE, MISO/MISO-OR, DICE/AXIS, KirHub
double differences, WTPAIR, and R-MAON already cover the mixed-difference
algebra, additive-nuisance removal, antisymmetric operators, or a direct
centered carrier. A panel of `L` ligands supplies at most `L-1` centered
reordering degrees of freedom. Pair or quartet expansion, folds, seeds, and
teacher-generated constraints do not increase independent biological `n`.

The still-untested object is narrower: an explicitly canonicalized
WT-to-single-substitution operation jointly acting with a recurring directed
ligand edit on continuous `Ki` or `Kd` rectangles from independent protein
families and provenance lineages.

## 3. Fixed Admission and Falsification Rules

Primary data require direct biochemical exact `Kd` or `Ki`, explicit WT and
single-mutant constructs, exact ligand parent connectivity, same-endpoint and
same-assay/context rectangles, and evidence location. Analyze `Kd` and `Ki`
separately.

Censored values, IC50, multi-mutations, indels, phosphorylation, and
cross-assay comparisons are sensitivity data. Cell viability, clinical
outcome, qualitative resistance, docking score, and simulated affinity are
excluded.

Freeze target, accession, base protein, homology, broad family, mutation
position, directed substitution, ligand, scaffold, chemical-neighbor,
directed ligand edit, assay, document, institution, site, and provenance
firewalls. Database names are not provenance units.

Any future label-bearing pretraining is fold-specific. Every derived
checkpoint, observation head, normalizer, data-selection model, hard-negative
rule, and feature projection inherits its training graph and is excluded with
held targets, homology/binding-profile components, ligands, scaffolds,
chemical neighbors, edits, assays, documents, sites, and provenance. Assay,
document, endpoint, and source variables may enter observation heads only,
never the deployable target-ligand interaction encoder.

Every protein coordinate enters separately:

1. position-only and aligned identity/one-hot;
2. pocket composition;
3. fixed BLOSUM and explicit physicochemical substitution delta;
4. pooled ESM-2 difference;
5. BLOSUM-label and directed-substitution-label permutations;
6. sequence and position shuffle;
7. parameter-matched random PSD;
8. matched wrong target/mutation and within-family wrong target/mutation.

The ligand/data battery includes ligand-only potency/identity, whole-molecule
difference, Morgan/physicochemical difference, explicit MMP edit, edit
permutation, quartet recombination, additive and random interaction nulls, and
document/assay/source-only prediction.

At C0, the primary paired contrast is true semantics versus the strongest
nested nonsemantic null under the identical estimator. Its component-blocked
95% lower confidence bound must exceed zero and the separately frozen
material-effect/MDE threshold. It must survive both protein and ligand
destruction and avoid worst-family/source collapse.

A failed coordinate or teacher stops. Bandwidth, rank, capacity, training
duration, seed count, threshold, fold, and coordinate/teacher concatenation
cannot rescue it.
