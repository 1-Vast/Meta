of `reports/active` on 2026-08-03. They are retained as scientific provenance,
not deleted. Historical prose may name the pre-move path; the family directory
below is the current location.

| `lexor/` | LEXOR evidence and transport probes. |
| `ubse/` | UBSE structure and evidence branches. |

machine-readable companions.
