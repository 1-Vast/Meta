Interaction Distillation**. It has three separable claims:

1. a PLIP-primary typed residue-FG event teacher can be measured reliably;

   FG marginals;

   dual-cold DTA.

biological event has been observed and no powered coupling claim exists.

## 4. Chemistry and cell universe

The FG vocabulary is event-role-scoped and multi-hot. It includes
hydrophobe, acceptor, donor, five/six-member aromatic ring, positive
ionizable, negative ionizable, and carbon-bound Cl/Br/I families. Graph
automorphisms use RDKit canonical ranks with ties unbroken, chirality and
isotopes included. No protomer/tautomer enumeration, neutralization, or
reionization is allowed.

PLIP charged-group ligand atom lists are not authoritative. Phosphate,
sulfate, guanidine, and related groups are reconstructed from `fgroup` and
the frozen canonical graph, followed by a unique coordinate-center match.
Ambiguous or unmapped events are null/unknown, never zero.

Formal-charge-supported and ionizable-heuristic events are reported
separately. Heuristic-only charge events cannot count as an independently
supported channel until their manual chemistry audit passes.

The future ledger contains one row for every legal cell for each extractor,
not only sparse positives. Its value is nullable `0/1/null`. A zero is legal
only when chemistry, atom resolution, mapping, and extractor completion all
pass and no primary PLIP event maps to the exact cell. Missing atoms,
nonunique mapping, extractor failure, and chemistry ineligibility are null.

Candidate residues are resolved polymer residues in the exact receptor auth
chain and selected model-1 altloc. The general event-blind pocket rule is a
resolved residue heavy atom within 6.5 A of a selected ligand heavy atom.
The water-only diagnostic envelope is 8.5 A. Sparse positive rows never
define the negative universe.

## 6. Candidate and informative tetrads

A candidate tetrad is defined entirely by the event-blind evaluability mask:
two residues, two FG orbits, four legal real-real cells, one complex, one
event channel, and no dustbin. It does not inspect `Y`.

After extraction, an informative tetrad may have either pattern:

```text
1 0      0 1
0 1  or  1 0
```

Its direction is `+1` or `-1`. Candidate and informative tetrads are never
interchanged. Event outcomes cannot select A1-C membership, promote a
reserve, choose an event, set a threshold, or define candidate rank.

The exact rational rank of the legal cycle matrix is used. Lexical greedy
selection fixes a unique independent basis. Cycle contributions are averaged
equally within a target unit and then equally across frozen dependency
components. Raw cycle count, extractor count, and derived tensor size are
not independent sample sizes.

## 7. Power correction

The historical gates of at least 200 A1-C targets with a legal checkerboard
and at least four event types on 50 targets each are coverage gates only.
They are not power gates.

For directional accuracy `0.60` versus `0.55`, one-sided Bonferroni
`0.05/7`, and 80% power:

```text
n=98:  critical successes 67, power 0.054711
n=512: critical successes 310, power 0.418954
minimum independent Bernoulli units for 80% power: 1065
```

Directional accuracy is therefore auxiliary and cannot produce a solo PASS.
The binding closure amendment selects component-held-out conditional tetrad
deviance gain against exact null `Omega=0`, with equal-target/channel and
component-macro aggregation, five
component-disjoint folds, Holm across channels, and max-T within registered
destructions. Its numeric material threshold and power curve must be
calibrated on A1-S TRAIN only, before A1-S development and A1-C release.
Until that calibration and the A1-S dependency certificate exist, there is
no powered claim.

## 9. Disposition of the supplied recommendations

Accepted now:

- no-P0A is the primary coupling arm;
- within-complex fixed-margin tetrads are the Stage-2 primary evidence;
- PLIP is the sole teacher and ProLIF is a non-voting audit;
- Level-0 placement and Level-1 chemical aggregates are diagnostics;
- Stage-4A remains `B0 + theta^T z_int`.

Deferred:

- a frozen pose ensemble is a credible later Stage-3 incremental arm because
  it adds deployment-available pair geometry;
- it first needs a separate license, training-membership, pocket/template,
  deployment-availability, interaction-recovery, weighting, and
  pose-destruction gate;
- it cannot replace the current no-P0A primary arm or enter Stage 1.

Rejected:

- calling the route counterfactual;
- PLIP/ProLIF consensus, union, or voting labels;
- treating hierarchical aggregates as substitutes for typed-channel passes;
- reselecting frozen A1-R/A1-S/A1-C members using realized events,
  checkerboards, log-determinants, or model outputs;
- using a flexible kernel to rescue failed information or power gates.
