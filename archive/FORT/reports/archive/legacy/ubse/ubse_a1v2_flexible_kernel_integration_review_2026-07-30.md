## 1. Binding conclusion

The useful lesson from *Flexible Kernels for Protein Property Prediction* is
that a trusted biological context can determine how observations share
statistical strength. It does not supply a new observation, an independent
component, or a ligand-conditioned residue-functional-group correspondence.

The A1-v2 order therefore does not change:

```text
Stage 1  extractor, chemistry, assembly, topology, and power
Stage 2  teacher coupling beyond the pair-conditioned rank-one null
Stage 3  deployment-side coupling student and semantic destruction gates
Stage 4A frozen 224-D linear residual, B0 + theta^T z_int
Stage 4B optional interaction-conditioned local residual kernel
```

Stage 4B is a delayed estimator candidate. It must be preregistered before
any Stage-4 affinity result is inspected, and it may execute only after
Stages 1-3 and the linear Stage-4A prototype pass. It cannot rescue a failed
information, topology, student, or linear-transfer gate.

This candidate is called an **interaction-conditioned local residual
kernel**. Calling it `ligand-conditioned CLOCK` would be premature: A1
observes wild-type residue-FG events, not the counterfactual effects of all
amino-acid substitutions required to identify a ligand-conditioned
substitution landscape.

## 3. What the paper establishes

The official arXiv page was independently checked on 2026-07-30:

- title: *Flexible Kernels for Protein Property Prediction*;
- authors: Martin Jankowiak, Yerdos Ordabayev, Rudraksh Tuwani, Henry N.
  Ward, Hunter Nisonoff, James M. McFarland, and Gevorg Grigoryan;
- arXiv `2606.11057`, version 1, submitted 2026-06-09;
- this review treats it as a preprint, not a peer-reviewed DTA result.

The paper's factual contribution and the present transfer hypothesis must
remain separate:

| Paper fact | Boundary for A1-v2 |
|---|---|
| LOCK combines a substitution-aware linear kernel with a local nonlinear-kernel times linear-kernel term. | This motivates a local residual on a certified A1 coordinate; it does not validate that coordinate. |
| Far from local sequence data, the nonlinear term decays and LOCK reverts to its extra linear predictor. | It does **not** automatically return to the DTA baseline `B0`. |
| CLOCK maps positional structure embeddings through a shared map to position-specific amino-acid correlation matrices. | A1 has no identified amino-acid substitution response and cannot yet support this claim. |
| The multi-task experiment uses 371 thermostability landscapes, with 280 training, 41 validation, and 50 test landscapes. | These are aligned local mutation landscapes with within-landscape labels, not jointly unseen target-ligand pairs at `k=0`. |
| Exact GP inference is used in small/moderate regimes; the paper discusses CG and approximate methods for larger data. | Exact full-DTA GP cost remains a separate feasibility problem and is not authorized here. |

The paper also states that fixed length or a common alignment is a current
kernel limitation. A 224-D deployment coupling coordinate avoids inventing
an alignment between unrelated targets, but it is then a deep interaction
kernel rather than CLOCK.

## 4. Mathematical corrections

### 4.1 The proposed linear-plus-local kernel does not return to B0

For a frozen shared coordinate `z`, nonnegative scales, and positive
lengthscale, the following is PSD:

```text
k_sum(i,j) =
    sigma_lin^2 * z_i^T z_j
  + sigma_loc^2
    * exp(-||z_i-z_j||^2 / (2 ell^2))
    * z_i^T z_j.
```

The linear Gram, RBF Gram, their Hadamard product, and their nonnegative sum
are PSD. But when the RBF term decays, the first term remains. The predictor
therefore reverts to a linear residual, not to `B0`.

The delayed local block will instead use

```text
z_bar(x) = D z_int(x)

k_loc(x,x') =
    1[endpoint(x) = endpoint(x')]
    * tau_endpoint^2
    * exp(-||z_bar(x)-z_bar(x')||^2 / (2 ell_endpoint^2))
    * z_bar(x)^T z_bar(x').
```

`D` must be a frozen, no-offset linear scaling. Mean subtraction, a bias, or
an added constant feature is forbidden because `z_int=0` must still map to
zero. The endpoint indicator is implemented by the already separate pKi and
pKd heads; endpoints are not pooled.

This local kernel is PSD by the Schur product theorem. It also satisfies
`k_loc(0,x)=0`. Its cross-covariance tends to zero as a query moves far from
all training support in the frozen `D z_int` metric. Strict dual-cold status
alone does not imply that distance, so event-space, target, scaffold, and
provenance support remain separate reported quantities.

### 4.2 A sample-wise PSD matrix is not a cross-sample kernel

The construction

```text
C_x = B_x B_x^T + epsilon I
```

proves only that each within-sample amino-acid matrix is PSD. It does not
prove that `K(x,x')` is symmetric or PSD across different protein-ligand
samples. A sample-specific factor also has the unresolved gauge
`B_x -> B_x Q_x`; using `B_x B_x'^T` is not meaningful unless a shared latent
axis has been independently fixed.

Any future matrix-valued route must expose a common feature map, for example
`vec(C_x)` with a Frobenius inner product, and pass a mixed-sample Gram test.
Numerical jitter belongs in the Gram solve, not in the biological feature
definition. This amendment does not authorize a `21 x 21`
ligand-conditioned substitution generator.

### 4.3 A GP variance-component null is nonregular

Setting a GP amplitude to zero is an algebraic return to the mean, but the
amplitude lies on a boundary and the lengthscale is unidentified under that
null. It would repeat the historical nonregular-null problem if ordinary
Wald or likelihood-ratio inference were used.

The primary comparison must use a frozen explicit feature map and directly
fitted coefficients. The exact null is then a regular zero coefficient
vector, and decisions use held-component performance differences rather
than an in-sample marginal-likelihood claim.

## 5. Delayed Stage-4B estimator

After a clean Stage-3 pass, define component-balanced landmarks `U` and
fixed Nyström features:

```text
phi_U(x) = (K_UU + eta I)^(-1/2) k_loc(U, x).
```

Landmarks, `D`, lengthscales, feature dimension, `eta`, and random seeds must
be frozen without affinity, within each legal TRAIN fold, and without query
batch information. The first paper-faithful extension is

```text
Stage 4A:
    y_hat_A(x,e) = B0(x,e) + theta_e^T z_int(x)

Stage 4B:
    y_hat_B(x,e) =
        B0(x,e) + theta_e^T z_int(x) + w_e^T phi_U(x).
```

The exact nested relations are:

```text
theta_e = 0 and w_e = 0  -> B0
w_e = 0                  -> Stage 4A
z_int(x) = 0             -> B0 for that sample
local cross-cov -> 0      -> Stage 4A, not B0
```

A finite-distance hard fallback to `B0` would require a separate frozen
TRAIN-only support gate multiplying the entire interaction residual. That
policy is not part of the current candidate and may not be introduced
without a coverage, selectivity, and calibration preregistration.

Exact GP/KRR on the full DTA table is not the first implementation. A
bounded Nyström or explicitly PSD random-feature prototype must first show:

- a Gram of the form `Phi Phi^T`;
- approximation error against exact small-block kernels;
- acceptable effective rank, condition number, and landmark concentration;
- a feature and parameter budget matched by direct and random controls;
- bounded wall time and peak memory on the available CUDA environment.

Posterior uncertainty is deferred. A future Bayesian feature head must be
calibrated by closed components and reported with support-stratified risk.
GP variance is not a substitute for target/scaffold/provenance/event-space
support or an OOD guarantee.

## 6. Affinity-blind kernel admission

The kernel may be preregistered for Stage 4B only after the Stage-3 student
exists and passes its original gates.

### K0: formal and implementation gate

Before event-based scoring:

- mixed-component Gram symmetry and normalized minimum eigenvalue at least
  `-1e-8`;
- exact `z_int=0 -> k_loc=0` and feature-zero tests;
- `theta=w=0` recovery of the same cross-fitted `B0` to machine precision;
- endpoint-block separation;
- synthetic remote-point cross-covariance decay;
- out-of-sample computation without query-batch normalization;
- Nyström/random-feature Gram remains explicitly `Phi Phi^T`;
- no sample-wise `B B^T`, asymmetric distance, affine recentering, or
  unregistered constant feature.

### K1: event-semantic gate

On A1-S component-held-out data, before affinity, freeze a score such as
negative component-macro legal-checkerboard cycle loss:

```text
S(K) = - component-held-out legal-checkerboard cycle loss.
```

The binding contrasts are:

```text
Delta_pair =
  S(K_true) - max(S(K_wrong_ligand), S(K_wrong_target))

Delta_local =
  S(K_loc) - max(S(K_linear), S(K_target x ligand),
                 S(K_target_only), S(K_ligand_only))

Delta_sem =
  S(K_loc) - max(S(K_FG_or_event_destroyed),
                 S(K_position_destroyed),
                 S(K_matched_random_PSD)).
```

Aligned identity, fixed BLOSUM, structure-only context, and their registered
permutations remain required controls. The previous LOCK-G0 result already
shows that fixed BLOSUM semantics were not identified in the earlier DTA
graph; a PSD pass cannot reverse that decision.

For every binding contrast:

- the closed-component bootstrap lower bound must exceed both zero and the
  preregistered material/MDE threshold;
- multiplicity across destruction contrasts must be controlled by a frozen
  max-T or Holm procedure;
- all registered seeds must have the same sign;
- no single component, family, source, or event type may dominate;
- cross-component support must be nontrivial; a near-identity Gram is a
  failure, not a safe success;
- wrong ligands must be size/FG/scaffold/ECFP matched so that chemical OOD
  alone cannot pass the test.

If the local kernel does not beat the linear coordinate and the strongest
separable/random controls, stop it. Increasing rank, landmarks, backbone,
training time, or seeds is not a reopening condition.

The A1-C role may be read only once after the Stage-3 student, the kernel,
all thresholds, and all controls are frozen. It cannot tune the kernel and
then be reused as confirmation.

## 7. Stage-4 admission and novelty boundary

Stage 4B must be preregistered before Stage-4A results are visible, but its
execution is conditional on Stage-4A PASS. In addition to every arm already
required by the parent blueprint, it must compare:

- the 224-D linear frozen-student head;
- the frozen local feature block;
- a parameter/budget-matched direct nonlinear head;
- linear RBF and matched random-PSD feature controls;
- target-only, ligand-only, and separable target-times-ligand kernels;
- wrong-target, wrong-ligand, FG/event/position-shuffled coordinates;
- zeroed and deleted local blocks.

Stage 4B has incremental value only if it beats Stage 4A by its own
pre-frozen material threshold and positive closed-component lower bound,
while also satisfying the parent DTA effect, RMSE, all-seed, dominance, and
destruction requirements. More landmarks do not create more independent
affinity components or reduce the empirical MDE.

The defensible conditional novelty is the complete chain:

> source- and membership-closed typed residue-FG coupling, a
> pair-conditioned exact null, a deployment-side coupling-only coordinate,
> and a preregistered local statistical-sharing rule with no target bypass.

GPs, BLOSUM kernels, structure-conditioned substitution matrices, pair
kernels, interaction fingerprints, low-rank PSD parameterizations, and
uncertainty estimates are not novel individually. Neither the paper nor this
amendment establishes strict dual-cold DTA gain.
