# LOCK/CLOCK G0 multi-agent synthesis

## Evidence layers

1. Jankowiak et al. show LOCK/CLOCK utility on fixed-length local protein mutation landscapes.
2. G0-L asks whether a BLOSUM substitution geometry is a distinct target coordinate in the kinase
   panel.
3. G0-R asks whether that coordinate transfers shared-ligand reorderings in a single-source,
   ligand-warm oracle.
4. Strict dual-cold prediction requires an independent multi-family factorial substrate.

No layer proves the next one.

## Independent audits

The historical/data agent found 372 label-free genes across 319 strict components and showed that
the fixed LOCK destruction battery had not previously been tested. It also established that the
family count gate was feasible but that empirical MDE, not target count, was binding.

The mathematical/adversarial agent proved the relevant PSD closure, required double-sided joint
nuisance residualization, identified arm-specific missingness and shared-profile dependence, and
required family-macro sensitivity. It also identified and forced closure of workbook-order,
eligibility-universe, seed, fold-map, helper-hash, and typed-artifact integrity channels.

The biological/novelty route separated global BLOSUM plausibility from position-specific or
structure-conditioned evidence. BLOSUM supplies conservative-substitution similarity but can remain
a family/taxonomy surrogate. KLIFS gives a defensible aligned kinase pocket, while no local Chroma,
SaProt, ProSST, Foldseek, or audited residue-environment asset exists for true CLOCK. A
conservation-weighted arm is therefore only a label-free position prior.

The model route retained one fixed nearest-profile estimator for every coordinate. No coordinate
concatenation or capacity increase was used. The direct-operator, scalar-gated, and product-kernel
carriers remained conditional designs because the information gate failed.

## Mathematical reading

For positive-semidefinite token correlation `C`, the positionwise linear kernel is a sum of PSD
kernels, and the nonlinear kernel is their Hadamard product. The fixed equal-scale LOCK kernel
`K_linear * (1 + K_nonlinear)` is PSD by Schur-product and sum closure; diagonal normalization
preserves PSD. The empirical token and target kernels passed the numerical PSD checks.

This mathematical validity does not imply that the BLOSUM labels are biologically load-bearing.
G0-R directly tested that distinction. Exact aligned identity beat LOCK, while BLOSUM-label
permutation and a median-correlation-matched random PSD token geometry were statistically
indistinguishable from or better than LOCK. The transferable signal is therefore alignment/target
specificity, not identified substitution semantics.

The local-linear fallback remains a reasonable prior for future mutation landscapes. It is not
identified as a DTA interaction prior on the present data. Matrix/tensor completion or a target-ligand
product kernel would inherit the same observational confounding without a complete factorial graph.

## Accepted conclusion

G0-L found a distinct but high-rank geometry. G0-R found some target-specific shared-panel signal but
failed the substitution-specific controls and the frozen substantive threshold. `conservation_LOCK`
showed a small label-free positional increment, not a structure-conditioned CLOCK result.

The accepted verdict is `LOCK_G0_REORDERING_NOT_IDENTIFIED_STOP`, mapping to final category 3.
The current data cannot identify the proposed mechanism and cannot authorize a predictive model.
