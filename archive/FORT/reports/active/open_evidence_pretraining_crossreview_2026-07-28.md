## 2. Established Evidence Versus New Hypotheses

### 2.1 Established in this project

1. Target-specific ligand reordering exists in some local panels. KirHub
   supplies strong ordinal mutation double differences, but it is kinase-only,
   ligand-warm, and one provenance lineage.
2. Correct aligned position, sequence, and target assignment can carry signal.
   Fixed BLOSUM semantics cannot currently be credited: LOCK lost to aligned
   identity and did not beat either BLOSUM-label permutation or matched random
   PSD geometry.
3. Pooled ESM-2 is not a reliable ligand-reordering coordinate on the audited
   substrates. This is substrate-specific evidence, not a universal PLM null.
4. Generic pretexts can be learned without improving downstream cold affinity.
   Measurement de-noising, pair retrieval, residue-teacher imitation, and GO
   supervision all demonstrated this mismatch.
5. Same-ligand target contrast is not automatically identified. TCOPA stopped
   for insufficient target-contrast support, and SAFSA's family-selectivity
   increment was only `+0.0047` with a negative family-bootstrap lower bound.
6. Pair, quartet, window, fold, seed, database-copy, and teacher expansion do
   not add independent biological units.
7. K-LBP's factorized null is nonregular. R-MAON's directly parameterized
   centered `Theta` has a unique regular null and passed synthetic calibration.
   This is estimator evidence only.
8. The current public graph lacks a powered, source-independent, multi-family,
   same-endpoint substrate with adequate shared-ligand depth. No real affinity
   training is authorized.

### 2.2 Hypotheses introduced or sharpened by the attachments

The following statements are hypotheses, not findings:

1. Endpoint-separated exact, censored, ordinal, and binary observation heads
   can learn a useful shared latent interaction without merging their meanings.
2. Excluding assay, document, and source variables from the interaction encoder
   is sufficient to prevent observation-process shortcuts.
3. Same-ligand cross-target selectivity supplies a protein-load-bearing
   pretraining target after family, assay, campaign, and provenance matching.
4. A frozen local WT-to-mutant coordinate learned from ProteinGym, MaveDB, or
   biophysical mutation tasks transfers to ligand-conditioned reordering.
5. AssayMatch-style selection reduces negative transfer beyond a simple
   endpoint and protocol filter.
6. Evidence recovery from original articles, supplements, and patents produces
   enough new independent complete rectangles to pass power.
7. D-optimal block acquisition improves coordinate discrimination without
   compromising inclusion-probability, source, or confirmation firewalls.
8. Binder2030 releases a usable matrix rather than only selected binders or a
   small public overlap subset.

None of these hypotheses has passed a project gate.

## 4. Frozen Same-Assay and Evidence Contract

The attachments sometimes use "comparable assay." That phrase is too weak for
the primary estimand. The following contract is frozen for any future primary
WT-mutant by ligand rectangle:

1. Exact direct biochemical `Kd` and exact direct biochemical `Ki` are separate
   strata. They are never numerically merged.
2. All four cells share one evidence-resolved assay identity and one protocol
   version or campaign. An aggregated assay-ID bundle, text similarity, or an
   AssayMatch score is not proof of sameness.
3. The construct sequence and numbering are explicit. The intervention is one
   canonical directed substitution from the registered WT construct.
4. Ligand parent connectivity and stereochemistry are exact and traceable.
   Salt stripping or parent normalization cannot merge different active forms.
5. Modality, buffer, pH, temperature, substrate/cofactor concentration,
   incubation, readout, and relevant competition conditions are identical or
   explicitly verified unchanged across the four cells.
6. The four cells come from one source/provenance lineage for a primary
   rectangle. An independent site must repeat a complete rectangle; cells from
   two sites cannot be stitched into one primary contrast.
7. The evidence location includes DOI or patent, supplement/table/figure,
   row/column or equivalent source coordinates, and the verbatim source value.
8. Exact values form the primary continuous analysis. Censored, inactive,
   failed-curve, BQL/AQL, IC50, percent-inhibition, and qualitative results are
   retained but enter separately specified observation or sensitivity heads.
9. Outcome-selected inclusion is prohibited unless a nonzero inclusion
   probability and weighting rule were frozen before value access.
10. Cross-assay rectangles, monotone mappings, or AssayMatch-selected records
    are sensitivity or pretraining evidence only. They cannot relax items 1-9.

This contract applies even if a multi-fidelity model fits a shared latent
variable. A latent scale does not make the original endpoints interchangeable.

## 5. Real-Label Training Prohibition and Fold-Specific Closure

### 5.1 Current prohibition

No real affinity, activity, censor, inactive, DMS outcome, or source-derived
teacher may currently be fitted. `OMUT-D0` permits source version, URL, rights,
size, hash, schema, and safe-projection work only. In particular:

- do not train MEDIP on current TRAIN, ChEMBL, BindingDB, Papyrus, Binder2030,
  Davis, KirHub, SPD, ToxCast, ProteinGym, or MaveDB outcomes;
- do not use an affinity-bearing global checkpoint as an initialization;
- do not inspect a value table while claiming a label-free topology audit;
- do not treat synthetic calibration as permission to read real outcomes.

After D0 and F0, D1 may use only a registered blind projector that drops
numeric value, relation, censor, inactive, and outcome fields before
materialization. Real-outcome modeling requires X0 and I0 to pass. A predictive
model additionally requires the C0 semantic gate.

### 5.2 Required closure for every downstream fold

If a later stage authorizes real-label pretraining, it must be rebuilt for each
downstream fold. For fold `f`:

1. Seed the exclusion graph with every held target, base protein, construct,
   family/homology component, binding-profile component, ligand parent,
   stereochemical entity, scaffold, chemical neighbor, directed edit, assay,
   document, institution/site, and provenance lineage.
2. Take transitive joint closure over target sequence/structure homology,
   family and binding-profile similarity; ligand identity, scaffold, MMP, and
   chemical-neighbor relations; and assay, document, database-copy, source,
   institution, and site relations.
3. Remove every pretraining observation and every derived artifact connected
   to that closure. Duplicate records from another database remain excluded.
4. Fit observation heads, assay weights, source offsets, normalizers, feature
   projections, hard-negative selection, bridge models, and hyperparameters
   only inside the remaining fold-TRAIN graph.
5. Never reuse a label-bearing global checkpoint across folds. A checkpoint is
   a derived data object and inherits all source and entity memberships of its
   training corpus.
6. Binding profiles may define blocks but may not enter the predictor.
7. For a primary ProteinGym/MaveDB transfer claim, remove held downstream
   target and homology-component mutation assays as well. A non-closed
   transductive arm may be reported separately but cannot support strict
   target-cold transfer.
8. Log source hashes, graph rules, connected exclusions, row counts, and all
   derived-artifact hashes before fitting.

Sequence-only self-supervision without biological outcome labels is a separate
exposure class, but model lineage and possible test-entity exposure must still
be disclosed. Affinity- or mutation-outcome pretraining is not made cold merely
by deleting its final prediction head.

## 6. Candidate-Specific Assessment

### 6.1 Evidence-bound OpenMut

**What it can mitigate:** missing construct, endpoint, assay, inclusion, and
provenance fields; database-copy inflation; unstructured WT-mutant panels.

**What it cannot do:** create measurements, create independent sources, convert
fitness or resistance into affinity, or infer missing numeric values.

**Minimum admissible record:** source version/hash, DOI/PMID/patent and exact
evidence location, institution/site, WT and mutant sequences, canonical
directed substitution, ligand parent/stereochemistry, endpoint/relation/value
and unit, assay conditions, replicate and inclusion metadata, provenance
lineage, extraction confidence, and verification status.

**Lowest-cost falsification:** complete `OMUT-D0`, then run D1 through a blind
metadata projector. Count complete candidate rectangles only after joint
base-protein, homology, family, assay, document, institution, and provenance
closure. Compute the optimistic/projected component MDE without reading values.

**Stop:** no safe projector, unresolved rights, or topology below the frozen
independent-unit and query-depth floor. More database mirrors do not reopen it.

**Reopen:** a new source supplies explicit constructs, same-assay shared
ligands, independent lineages, retained non-hits, and enough closed components
to power the registered effect.

### 6.2 MEDIP observation and interaction pretraining

An admissible abstract form is:

```text
eta(t,l) = F(t,l)
y(t,l,a,s,e) ~ p_e(y | g_e(eta), assay=a, source=s)
```

`eta` may depend only on deployable target and ligand inputs. Endpoint, assay,
document, site, and source may enter only the observation head.

This separation is necessary but not sufficient. If family occurs in only one
assay or source, the observation head and `eta` remain statistically
confounded. Independent bridge cells and graph closure, not architecture
alone, identify the decomposition. Flexible monotone heads can also absorb the
interaction or make latent scales arbitrary.

The smallest useful implementation is not a new backbone. It is one fixed
interaction encoder plus:

- separate exact Kd and exact Ki heads;
- explicit interval likelihoods for censoring;
- separate ordinal or binary heads for lower-fidelity outcomes;
- a same-ligand matched target-selectivity loss;
- assay/source inputs prohibited from `F`;
- a direct centered downstream `Theta` with `Theta=0` as the exact null.

**Synthetic-only falsification:** under a registered simulator, compare the
correct observation model against forced endpoint merging, shuffled
endpoint/source, source injected into the interaction encoder, shuffled
selectivity, a ligand-only/separable null, and the direct-null model. Require
null calibration and recovery of the known interaction. This earns engineering
credit only.

**Real-data topology falsification:** before outcomes, count same-ligand
cross-target blocks that share endpoint and experimental context, within-family
matched target contrasts, source-independent repeats, and mutation rectangles.
Report independent components and MDE, not pair counts.

**Stop:** the same-ligand topology is sparse; target and assay/source are
confounded; protein shuffle or within-family wrong target does not destroy the
gain; endpoint merging performs equally; or gain is only calibration/RMSE
without mixed-difference improvement.

**Reopen:** an X0/I0-qualified graph contains powered same-assay target
contrasts across families and sources, and fold-specific closure is executable.

### 6.3 ProteinGym/MaveDB local mutation-coordinate pretraining

The useful candidate is a directional local coordinate:

```text
u(m) = Pool_[r in N(p)](E(mut)_r - E(WT)_r)
```

where position `p`, direction, neighborhood, and structure availability are
frozen. ProteinGym/MaveDB outcomes usually measure fitness, expression,
stability, or function. They may teach a mutation operation; they are not
pseudo-affinity labels.

The major scientific risk is task mismatch. A coordinate can predict generic
variant damage while failing to predict which ligand is reordered. Direction
antisymmetry and local structure prediction are useful constraints but do not
establish ligand-conditioned semantics.

Coordinates must enter one at a time in the same fixed estimator:

1. aligned identity or position-only;
2. fixed BLOSUM;
3. local PLM delta;
4. structure-conditioned delta, only at the later structure gate;
5. pooled ESM difference;
6. composition;
7. substitution-label permutation;
8. sequence/position shuffle;
9. parameter-matched random PSD;
10. within-family wrong mutation and wrong target.

**Lowest-cost falsification:** D0 metadata/rights and label-free coverage audit.
Measure whether substitution classes recur across closed base proteins,
families, and sources and whether held-family coordinates are nondegenerate.
Do not train the coordinate until its stage is registered and authorized.

**Stop:** the coordinate is predictable from family/composition, collapses
under held-family transfer, does not beat identity/random PSD in C0, or its
benefit survives mutation-label or target corruption.

**Reopen:** a frozen outcome-independent coordinate and a powered I0 substrate
support the full semantic destruction battery. Structure conditioning also
requires the later structure gate and a parameter-matched structure shuffle.

### 6.4 AssayMatch and same-ligand selectivity

AssayMatch is admissible only as train-data selection. It cannot certify that
two records form one primary assay. The required comparison is:

```text
all eligible TRAIN data
vs endpoint-only filter
vs frozen manual protocol filter
vs generic assay-text similarity
vs fold-TRAIN attribution-calibrated AssayMatch
vs assay-description shuffle
```

Same-ligand target selectivity is useful because a ligand-only score is
constant within a contrast. It can still be solved by family, campaign,
assay-threshold, target popularity, or active-only selection. Prefer
within-family or close-homology targets measured for the same ligand in the
same campaign with retained tested negatives and known inclusion.

**Lowest-cost falsification:** label-free contingency tables of ligand by
target by exact assay/campaign by family by provenance. Then test whether a
simple endpoint/protocol rule leaves enough closed components before fitting
any text encoder.

**Stop:** AssayMatch does not beat the simple frozen filter; text shuffle does
not remove its effect; selected assays leak downstream labels; or selectivity
does not beat family/source and within-family wrong-target controls.

**Reopen:** a source-resolved graph contains enough matched tested target
contrasts, and assay selection is learnable entirely in inner TRAIN folds.

### 6.5 D-optimal prospective blocks

D-optimal design can spend a fixed assay budget efficiently. It cannot rescue
an unidentifiable initial model and must not replace randomized confirmation.

The admissible order is:

1. run the complete randomized two-site A0 reliability/variance pilot;
2. freeze candidate coordinates and estimate noise/censoring from A0;
3. choose complete mini-blocks, such as two constructs by four ligands, rather
   than isolated outcome-selected cells;
4. optimize information for contrasts among candidate coordinates and
   `Theta`, not discovery of strong binders;
5. record nonzero inclusion probabilities and keep adaptive blocks
   training-only;
6. retain an independent randomized confirmation lineage.

**Lowest-cost falsification:** simulate the frozen candidate design using the
A0 covariance and missingness envelope. Compare D-optimal block acquisition
with uniform random block acquisition for minimum eigenvalue, parameter MDE,
family balance, and worst-source balance.

**Stop:** A0 reordering is not cross-site reproducible; the information matrix
remains rank-deficient; selection collapses to one family/scaffold; inclusion
probabilities become zero; or random blocks perform equivalently within the
registered margin.

**Reopen:** more independent families, larger complete blocks, a revised
randomized tranche, or an independent site changes the information topology.
Changing the neural model is not a reopening event.

### 6.6 Binder2030 citation conflict

The local RAMCI S0 audit recorded Binder2030 as "not a real/indexed dataset."
That statement is now superseded on indexability:

- Crossref resolves DOI `10.1016/j.slasd.2026.100299`, title *Binder2030: a
  quantitative membrane proteome binding dataset enabling AI-driven drug
  discovery*, published April 2026.
- PubMed resolves PMID `41740721`, PII `S2472-5552(26)00005-5`, and records it
  as indexed for MEDLINE.
- The PubMed abstract states 3,384 small-molecule ligands across approximately
  400 transmembrane proteins with standardized Kd measurements.
- The abstract says a PubChem-overlap subset is released. It does not establish
  release of the full matrix, complete tested negatives, inclusion
  probabilities, shared-ligand density, assay-level metadata, independent
  sources, or model-training rights.
- Crossref exposes CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 for the article version of record. That is
  not evidence that a separate data release permits model training or
  redistribution.

The attachment is therefore correct that the paper exists, but it oversteps
the available evidence if it treats Binder2030 as an already admissible DTA
substrate.

**Disposition:** add Binder2030 only as an `OMUT-D0` source candidate. Freeze
the dataset URL/version, file list, hashes, data license, schema, target and
ligand identifiers, exact endpoint fields, tested-negative retention,
inclusion mechanism, matrix density, assay/campaign fields, and source
lineages. No Binder2030 value access or training is authorized by this review.

**Stop:** only binders or the small overlap subset are public, non-hits are
absent, the graph lacks shared ligands, or rights/provenance are unresolved.

**Reopen:** a versioned legal release supplies complete or probability-known
tested cells with enough multi-family shared-ligand depth after closure.

## 7. Dilemma-by-Dilemma Falsification Map

### 7.1 Target extrapolation and the affinity baseline

- **Mitigation:** interaction-aligned selectivity/mixed-difference pretraining,
  with explicit ligand-only and shared-global terms.
- **Existing evidence:** de-noising and several successful pretexts did not
  improve cold-target affinity; unseen-target baseline remains unidentified.
- **Lowest-cost falsification:** label-free same-ligand target-contrast
  topology, then protein-shuffle and shared-global null on an authorized fold.
- **Stop/reopen:** stop if the protein channel is removable or only improves
  measurement fit; reopen with independent target contrasts and a powered
  target baseline bridge.

### 7.2 Rectangle collapse in real substrates

- **Mitigation:** evidence-bound OpenMut recovery and Binder2030 D0 audit.
- **Existing evidence:** Davis has about 10-11 base proteins; KirHub is one
  source; CROSSDOC has 11 strict homology components.
- **Lowest-cost falsification:** blind-projected counts of exact constructs,
  same-assay shared ligands, families, sources, and complete components.
- **Stop/reopen:** stop below query-depth and component floors; reopen only
  with newly resolved complete records or prospective blocks.

### 7.3 Power, pseudoreplication, and unstable statistics

- **Mitigation:** component-level MDE, block acquisition, and direct regular
  operators; never count pairs as independent.
- **Existing evidence:** the optimistic `0.03` mechanism floor needs about 88
  independent components; strict prediction remains about 423, while several
  historical statistics were denominator-unstable.
- **Lowest-cost falsification:** graph-closed independent `n`, query depth,
  effective rank, design effect, and projected MDE before outcomes.
- **Stop/reopen:** stop when the registered effect is below MDE; reopen with
  more independent components or deeper randomized ligand panels, not more
  pairs, folds, or seeds.

### 7.4 Family, document, assay, and provenance leakage

- **Mitigation:** Open Evidence Graph, assay/source observation heads, and
  fold-specific transitive closure.
- **Existing evidence:** RECRO nominal cross-document signal collapsed after
  provenance-family isolation; group centroid beat protein coordinates.
- **Lowest-cost falsification:** predict labels or inclusion from family,
  assay, document, and source alone; compare correct target with matched
  within-family wrong target.
- **Stop/reopen:** stop if any source-only/taxonomy arm explains the gain or
  independent lineages vanish; reopen with genuinely independent sites and
  cross-family repeats.

### 7.5 Endpoint incompatibility, censoring, and selection

- **Mitigation:** endpoint-separated likelihoods, interval censoring, retained
  non-hits, and frozen inclusion probabilities.
- **Existing evidence:** exact Kd/Ki separation is already binding; current
  multi-fidelity I1 remains untested.
- **Lowest-cost falsification:** synthetic observation-head calibration plus
  D1 metadata counts by endpoint, relation, and inclusion mechanism without
  outcome materialization.
- **Stop/reopen:** stop if forced endpoint merge is indistinguishable, heads
  are unidentifiable, or non-hits/inclusion are missing; reopen with a
  randomized or probability-known panel and endpoint-specific bridges.

### 7.6 Ligand-only, scaffold, and chemical-edit shortcuts

- **Mitigation:** same-ligand target contrasts, ligand-only/shared-global
  nested nulls, and separate ligand coordinate arms.
- **Existing evidence:** generic ligand potency is the strongest stable signal;
  local edits and DICE did not transfer; a ligand giant component dominates
  some public graphs.
- **Lowest-cost falsification:** ligand-only, whole-molecule, MMP, scaffold,
  edit-shuffle, and within-family wrong-target controls under identical data.
- **Stop/reopen:** stop if protein destruction leaves performance unchanged or
  one scaffold/component dominates; reopen with shared scaffold-diverse
  ligands and recurring edits across independent proteins and sources.

### 7.7 Protein coordinates and semantic non-identification

- **Mitigation:** a frozen local ProteinGym/MaveDB mutation delta tested one
  coordinate at a time.
- **Existing evidence:** fixed LOCK does not beat identity, BLOSUM permutation,
  or random PSD; true CLOCK and local mutation deltas are untested.
- **Lowest-cost falsification:** label-free family/composition predictability,
  held-family nondegeneracy, and then the fixed C0 destruction battery.
- **Stop/reopen:** stop if identity/random geometry is as good or corruption
  does not destroy signal; reopen with an externally frozen coordinate plus a
  powered multi-family source-resolved substrate.

### 7.8 Estimator and null identifiability

- **Mitigation:** optimize centered `Theta` directly; use endpoint heads only
  around that regular carrier.
- **Existing evidence:** K-LBP is nonregular at `gamma=0`; R-MAON passes
  synthetic null calibration and recovery.
- **Lowest-cost falsification:** reuse registered null, active,
  heteroscedastic, degenerate, and null-coordinate simulations for MEDIP's
  complete loss.
- **Stop/reopen:** stop for type-I error, recovery, or null-identity failure;
  reopen by correcting the estimator, not by adding real data or extra seeds.

### 7.9 Structure, pose, and privileged teachers

- **Mitigation:** keep structure out of the current route; later test one
  frozen structure-conditioned mutation coordinate, not docking affinity.
- **Existing evidence:** docking, holo increment, native-pose, and physics
  additions failed strong ligand controls; no tau teacher is admissible.
- **Lowest-cost falsification:** rights/input/overlap audit and pair-specific
  nondegeneracy with pose/interface shuffle before labels.
- **Stop/reopen:** stop if the teacher requires unavailable test-time inputs,
  overlaps held pairs, or fails destruction; reopen only after OMUT-M1/R0 with
  a new legal frozen structure map and parameter-matched shuffle.

### 7.10 Tau-style future supervision

- **Mitigation:** use only verified intervention-conditioned delta supervision;
  MEDIP does not create a future state by itself.
- **Existing evidence:** current TRAIN lacks canonical bilateral interventions
  and has zero admissible pair-specific teachers.
- **Lowest-cost falsification:** G0 topology and T0 teacher-contract audits
  before any synthetic student.
- **Stop/reopen:** existing G0/T0 stops stand; reopen only with canonical
  recurring interventions and a frozen legal pair-specific teacher, not more
  generated constraints.

### 7.11 Distinct evaluation targets

- **Mitigation:** report observation fit, coordinate semantics, reordering,
  strict prediction, calibration, and power as separate gates.
- **Existing evidence:** several representation and measurement wins failed
  downstream; synthetic R-MAON does not establish biology.
- **Lowest-cost falsification:** require the chain `observation model ->
  semantic destruction -> mixed difference -> strict paired gain`, with a
  separate metric at each link.
- **Stop/reopen:** stop advancement at the first failed link; reopen only that
  link with new information. A lower-level pass never substitutes for a
  higher-level result.

### 7.12 Rights and reader boundaries

- **Mitigation:** D0 rights/version/hash registry, blind projectors, and
  evidence-bound extraction.
- **Existing evidence:** Davis use is irreversible; PLATINUM, MdrDB,
  BindingDB, ChEMBL, and Binder2030 each have unresolved route-specific
  boundaries.
- **Lowest-cost falsification:** verify licenses, source URLs, checksums,
  schemas, redistribution/training terms, and projector behavior without
  opening outcomes.
- **Stop/reopen:** stop any source lacking legal use or safe projection; reopen
  only after terms and reproducible access are frozen.

## 8. Ordered Route Recommendation

1. **Complete `OMUT-D0`; do nothing else with real outcomes.** Add source
   metadata audits for Binder2030, ProteinGym/MaveDB, and any proposed assay
   corpus. Freeze rights, versions, URLs, sizes, hashes, schemas, and safe
   projectors.
2. **Complete `OMUT-F0` and the irreversible Davis decision.** No source can
   silently move between development and confirmation.
3. **Run label-free `OMUT-D1`.** Audit canonical mutation rectangles,
   same-ligand target contrasts, recurring directed ligand edits, independent
   families/provenance, effective rank, query depth, and MDE. This is the main
   kill gate for every proposed pretraining route.
4. **If D1 fails, stop public-data pretraining.** Run the randomized two-site
   prospective A0 reliability/variance pilot. Consider D-optimal complete
   blocks only after A0 identifies reproducible reordering and covariance.
5. **If D1 passes, build `OMUT-X0`, then run I0.** Recover evidence-bound
   outcomes and establish reliability, selection, reordering variation, and
   empirical power before fitting any representation.
6. **After I0 only, preregister two separate candidate experiments.** One is a
   synthetic MEDIP observation-model calibration; the other is a frozen local
   mutation-coordinate arm. Do not concatenate them and do not credit
   synthetic success as biology.
7. **At C0, test coordinates one at a time in one fixed estimator.** If
   synthetic MEDIP calibration passes, its real fold-specific pretraining may
   enter as one separately registered coordinate arm at this stage. Require
   endpoint separation, assay/source exclusion, same-ligand selectivity
   ablations, full fold closure, and true semantics to beat identity, family,
   composition, pooled ESM, BLOSUM permutation, random PSD, and matched wrong
   target/mutation.
8. **Only a C0-passing coordinate may enter the direct operator.** Run the
   smallest M0/M1 carrier with exact nested ligand-only and shared-global nulls.
9. **Reserve performance claims for M1/T1 and independent confirmation.** A
   result must reproduce across families and provenance, survive target and
   ligand corruption, and exceed the frozen material effect with adequate
   power.

The most promising new research object is not a larger affinity backbone. It
is a frozen, local, directional mutation coordinate tested inside a
source-resolved, fold-closed interaction-pretraining system. That object is
scientifically plausible and still untested. It is not currently trainable.

## 9. Final Decision

The proposals improve the research program in three ways:

1. they sharpen OpenMut into an evidence-recovery system rather than a database
   merge;
2. they define a falsifiable local mutation-coordinate hypothesis rather than
   reusing pooled protein identity;
3. they specify how heterogeneous outcomes might be used without declaring
   Kd, Ki, IC50, censoring, and binary activity interchangeable.

They do not show that the recovered graph is powered, that the local mutation
coordinate carries ligand-conditioned semantics, that MEDIP avoids taxonomy
and provenance shortcuts, or that any mechanism improves strict dual-cold DTA.
